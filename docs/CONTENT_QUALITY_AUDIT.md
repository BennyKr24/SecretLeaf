# SecretLeaf — Content Quality Audit (Phase 16)

> Phase 16 — Content Quality Overhaul. No new database, search, or infrastructure
> work. This audit evaluates **content quality only** and defines the roadmap to
> turn every article into a professional cannabis cultivation reference rather
> than a blog post.
>
> Owner: Editorial & Agronomy · Status: Active · Date: 2026-06-02 · Version: 1.0

Companion documents:
- `docs/CANNABIS_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md` — the binding editorial framework (voice,
  evidence policy, canonical 16-block article structure). This audit measures the
  live corpus against that standard.
- `docs/KNOWLEDGE_OS_REPORT.md` — Knowledge OS foundation (Phases 0–15).

---

## 1. Executive summary

The knowledge corpus is defined in `apps/web/src/data/terpira/wiki.ts` as
`TerpiraArticle` records. **82 article definitions** exist, but only the **37**
articles registered in the curated `GROW_KNOWLEDGE` allowlist are actually
exported and surfaced to users (`wikiArticles` filters on
`a.slug in GROW_KNOWLEDGE`). The remaining **45 definitions are orphaned** — they
exist in source but never reach a reader.

Measured against the Phase 4 canonical structure in
`docs/CANNABIS_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md`, the corpus has three systemic problems:

1. **Structural drift.** No article uses the mandated 16-block schema
   (`definition`, `scientific_background`, `plant_physiology`, `symptoms`,
   `causes`, `diagnosis`, `corrective_actions`, `preventive_measures`,
   `environmental_factors`, `nutrient_interactions`, `common_mistakes`,
   `advanced_considerations`, `related_topics`, `references`, `faq`,
   `expert_tips`). Live articles use free-form `sections[]` plus `faq`,
   `glossary`, `warnings`, and `keyTakeaways`. The strongest articles cover ~5–7
   equivalents of these blocks; the weakest cover 2.
2. **A long tail of generic, templated "lite" articles.** 27 articles are
   generated by `createLiteArticle` from boilerplate (identical two-section
   skeleton, placeholder copy such as *"Dieser Beitrag ordnet das Thema … in
   einen belastbaren Rahmen ein"*). These read like SEO filler, not a cultivation
   handbook.
3. **Evidence and diagnostics gaps.** The editorial standard's own worked example
   ("Magnesium Deficiency") has **no corresponding article**. There are no
   per-nutrient deficiency / diagnostic entries — the single closest article is
   `naehrstoffblockaden-und-antagonismen`, which is a lite article.

**Corpus quality distribution** (Content Quality score, see §2):

| Tier | Score band | Articles | Share |
|------|-----------|----------|-------|
| A — handbook-grade | 80–100 | 7 | 9% |
| B — solid, minor gaps | 65–79 | 8 | 10% |
| C — usable, structurally thin | 45–64 | 25 | 30% |
| D — blog-grade / templated | < 45 | 42 | 51% |

Mean score: **49.5 / 100**. Median tier: **D**. The headline conclusion: roughly
half the corpus does not yet meet the "professional cultivation handbook"
bar set by the editorial standard.

## 2. Methodology

### 2.1 Audit dimensions

Every article was evaluated against the six dimensions required by the Phase 16
brief, interpreted through the editorial standard:

| Dimension | What was checked |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Scientific accuracy** | Mechanistic explanation present; claims quantified with units; uncertainty flagged where evidence is weak. |
| **Horticultural accuracy** | Correct domain terminology (substrate, rhizosphere, transpiration, VPD/PPFD/DLI, antagonism, chlorosis); cultivation thresholds in ranges. |
| **Terminology consistency** | Consistent DE/EN technical register; no "strain" where "cultivar" is meant; abbreviations expanded on first use. |
| **Diagnostic usefulness** | Symptoms → causes → diagnosis → corrective actions chain; differential reasoning; measurement procedure. |
| **Actionability** | Concrete, measurable interventions (checklists, thresholds, protocols) over generic advice. |
| **Completeness** | Coverage of the canonical 16-block skeleton; presence of FAQ, references, glossary, expert guidance. |

### 2.2 Content Quality score (0–100)

A transparent, reproducible rubric was applied to each article using its source
structure (sections, checklists, warnings, FAQ, glossary, sources, curation
status). This is **distinct** from the existing `qualityScore` field in
`GROW_KNOWLEDGE`, which measures *grow-signal strength* (1–5), not editorial
completeness.

| Component | Weight | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Structural depth (section count) | 30 | Proxy for coverage of the canonical block skeleton. |
| Actionability (checklist present) | 12 | Measurable interventions vs. prose only. |
| Risk guidance (warning present) | 6 | Safety / failure-mode coverage. |
| FAQ (≥2 Q&A) | 10 | Maps to `faq` block + FAQ schema. |
| Glossary (≥3 terms) | 8 | Terminology discipline. |
| Evidence (source count, capped at 4) | 14 | Citations per evidence policy (§3 of standard). |
| Curation / export status | 12 | Whether the article is actually published. |
| Grow-signal (`qualityScore` scaled) | 8 | Editorial priority of the topic. |
| Templated "lite" penalty | −22 | Generic boilerplate is not handbook-grade. |

Scores were computed programmatically from `wiki.ts`; the full ranking is in §3.

## 7. Biggest content gaps

Topics a professional cultivation reference must cover that are **missing or
inadequate** today:

1. **Per-nutrient deficiency / toxicity articles (diagnostics).** The editorial
   standard's own worked example is "Magnesium Deficiency", yet **no deficiency
   article exists**. Missing, at minimum: nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium,
   calcium, magnesium, iron, and the canonical lockout/excess pairs. This is the
   single largest gap and the best fit for the 16-block diagnostic schema.
2. **Structured diagnostic decision support.** No symptom-led troubleshooting
   path (leaf-symptom → differential → measurement → correction). The platform
   has the block types (`symptoms`, `causes`, `diagnosis`) but no article uses
   them.
3. **Environmental control depth.** VPD is covered, but temperature/RH targets by
   stage, DLI/photoperiod, and CO₂ enrichment lack dedicated, quantified entries.
4. **IPM specifics.** `integrierte-schaedlingspraevention-grow` exists, but there
   are no pest/pathogen-specific entries (spider mites, thrips, powdery mildew,
   botrytis) with identification and staged response.
5. **Harvest-window determination.** Trichome maturity is mentioned inside
   biosynthesis copy but lacks a standalone harvest-timing reference.
6. **Published medical/legal/market coverage.** 19 orphaned articles mean three
   whole categories are invisible. Decide per article: uplift-and-publish or
   deprecate to keep the corpus focused on cultivation.

## 8. Structure & language findings

### 8.1 Structure standardization gap

The target is the canonical 16-block schema in
`docs/CANNABIS_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md §4`. Current mapping of the legacy
`TerpiraArticle` shape:

| Canonical block | Present in live data? | Notes |
|-----------------|----------------------|-------|
| `definition` | Partial | First section sometimes acts as a definition; not typed/guaranteed. |
| `scientific_background` | Rare | Only the strongest articles include mechanism. |
| `plant_physiology` | Rare | Largely absent outside top-tier articles. |
| `symptoms` | Missing | No typed symptom block anywhere. |
| `causes` | Missing | Causal ranking not modeled. |
| `diagnosis` | Missing | No differential/measurement procedure. |
| `corrective_actions` | Partial | Approximated by `checklist` arrays. |
| `preventive_measures` | Partial | Approximated by `checklist`/`warnings`. |
| `environmental_factors` | Partial | Strong in VPD articles, ad hoc elsewhere. |
| `nutrient_interactions` | Missing | No interaction/antagonism modeling. |
| `common_mistakes` | Rare | Occasionally inside prose, never typed. |
| `advanced_considerations` | Rare | — |
| `related_topics` | Present | `relatedSlugs[]`. |
| `references` | Partial | `sourceIds[]`; lite articles use category defaults only. |
| `faq` | Present | `faq[]`. |
| `expert_tips` | Missing | `keyTakeaways`/`growValue` are adjacent but not expert-tip blocks. |

**Conclusion:** the corpus carries ~6 of 16 blocks on its best days and ~3 on its
worst. Standardization means authoring the missing diagnostic and interaction
blocks, not reformatting existing prose.

### 8.2 Language findings

Against the standard's Prohibited/Required lists (§2 of the editorial standard):

- **Generic blog phrasing** is concentrated in the 27 lite articles — boilerplate
  like *"Dieser Beitrag ordnet das Thema … in einen belastbaren Rahmen ein"* and
  *"Kurz erklaert: Worum geht es hier?"* This is exactly the SEO-filler register
  the standard prohibits.
- **Beginner hand-holding** appears in `simpleExplainers` ("Kurz erklaert …"),
  which is acceptable as a presentation aid but must not replace mechanistic
  copy.
- **Top-tier articles already model the target register** — quantified ranges
  (VPD 1.2–1.6 kPa, aw < 0.65, PPFD variance < 20%), correct terminology, and
  source anchoring. The uplift is to bring the rest up to this bar, not to invent
  a new voice.
- **Terminology consistency** is generally good in the cultivation core; drift is
  mostly in the orphaned medical/market lite articles.

## 9. Improvement roadmap

Sequenced for maximum reader impact first, with no database/search/infra changes
(per the Phase 16 mandate). All work targets `apps/web/src/data/terpira/wiki.ts`
content and the editorial standard.

### Wave 1 — Stop shipping blog-grade content (highest ROI)
- Rewrite the **5 published lite articles** (#34–38) into full, 16-block-aligned
  articles: `naehrstoffbedarf-cannabis-lebenszyklus`, `stressmarker-frueh-erkennen`,
  `substrat-vergleich-coco-erde-hydro`, `indoor-outdoor-anbau-vergleich`,
  `naehrstoffblockaden-und-antagonismen`.
- For each: add quantified thresholds, a comparison/interaction table, explicit
  `corrective_actions`/`preventive_measures`, and article-specific `sourceIds`
  (no category-default fallback).
- **Exit criterion:** no exported article is `Lite`; every published article ≥ 60 CQ.

### Wave 2 — Uplift published tier-C core grow articles to A/B
- Targets: `cannabis-substrat-und-wurzelzone`, `bewaesserung-ohne-uebergiessen`,
  `lichtstress-und-canopy-management`, `schimmel-und-mykotoxine-bei-cannabis`,
  `grow-log-und-kpi-dashboard`, `feminisiert-vs-regular-vs-autoflower` (§6).
- Add `scientific_background`, `plant_physiology`, `diagnosis`, and `expert_tips`
  blocks; expand sources to ≥ 3 article-specific records.
- **Exit criterion:** Anbau & Ernte category mean ≥ 75.

### Wave 3 — Close the diagnostics gap (new content)
- Author the per-nutrient **deficiency/toxicity** article set (N, P, K, Ca, Mg,
  Fe + lockout pairs) using the standard's "Magnesium Deficiency" worked example
  as the template, and a symptom-led troubleshooting hub.
- Add IPM pest/pathogen-specific entries (spider mites, thrips, powdery mildew,
  botrytis) and a harvest-window article.
- **Exit criterion:** every leaf-symptom a grower can observe resolves to a
  diagnostic article via `related_topics`.
